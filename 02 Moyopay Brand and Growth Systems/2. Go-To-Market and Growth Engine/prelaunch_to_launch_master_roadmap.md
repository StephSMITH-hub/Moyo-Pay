# 2. PHASE 1: THE ACTIVATING STORY & NARRATIVE SEEDING (WEEKS 1 TO 4)

* **Objective**: Introduce the core narrative wound without premature product selling. Establish Moyopay as the intellectual authority on African currency sovereignty.

### Weekly Sprint Deliverables:
* **Week 1 (Narrative Grounding)**: Publish the foundational founder essay on Substack and X: *"Why holding local currency savings in 2026 is an active financial emergency."*
* **Week 2 (The News-Jacking Engine)**: React to monthly inflation numbers and Central Bank FX circulars within 90 minutes. Deploy the "Receipts" comparison cards showing real purchasing power decay over five years.
* **Week 3 (The Freelancer & Founder Focus)**: Run targeted threads addressing cross-border client invoicing delays and card decline frustrations.
* **Week 4 (The Retargeting Kickoff)**: Activate the $30 Meta ad retargeting experiment ($1.00/day) serving custom video snippets to non-converting visitors of `moyopay.io`.

# 3. PHASE 2: PRIVATE ALPHA TESTING, TRUST & LEADERBOARD (WEEKS 5 TO 8)

* **Objective**: Prove mechanistic reality through real user transactions, kill market cynicism, and turn passive subscribers into active recruiters.

### Weekly Sprint Deliverables:
* **Week 5 (The Reawakening & Alpha Intake)**:
  - Send Reactivation Email 1 (`pioneer #0001: your dollar wallet position is confirmed`) to all 1,400 existing subscribers.
  - Screen, verify, and invite the 100-person Private Alpha Cohort into the Telegram War Room.
  - Target: Hit 2,100 verified signups.
* **Week 6 (Mechanistic Proof & Trust Armor)**:
  - Send Reactivation Email 2 (`under the hood: how 14 seconds actually happens`).
  - Publish the 6-Slide Trust Carousel documenting CBN-licensed fiat rails, 1:1 US Treasury reserve backing, and Visa/Mastercard card issuing.
  - Run Alpha Drill 1 and Drill 2 (simulated client invoicing and wallet funding).
  - Target: Hit 2,900 verified signups.
* **Week 7 (The Pioneer Referral Rollout)**:
  - Deploy the 3-Tier Milestone Unlock Leaderboard (`Tier 1: Card Priority`, `Tier 2: 90-Day Fee Holiday`, `Tier 3: Top 50 Inner Circle Metal Card`).
  - Send Referral Email (`skip 500 spots in line`).
  - Launch the UGC prompt: *"What did the naira cost you this year?"*
  - Target: Hit 3,900 verified signups.
* **Week 8 (The Pre-Launch Climax)**:
  - Announce the Top 50 Pioneer Leaderboard winners.
  - Verify and cross the 5,000-subscriber milestone.
  - Complete the final Private Alpha bug audits and security penetration tests.
  - Target: Reach 5,000+ verified waitlist members.
