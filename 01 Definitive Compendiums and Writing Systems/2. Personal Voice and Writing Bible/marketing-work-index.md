## Profile & Working Philosophy
- Nigeria-based strategic marketing professional; identifies as a "Narrative Systems Strategist" — works at the intersection of brand strategy, narrative design, content systems, and marketing operations.
- Thinks in layers: **industry shift → market behavior → psychology → positioning → messaging → content → distribution.** Builds systems, not one-off solutions; documentation is treated as organizational infrastructure.
- Standard working method: **full draft first → sharp/pointed feedback → clean rebuild** — never incremental patching.
- On methodology-sensitive engagements (BCP, ZendPay): **systematic and question-first — never impulsive output before clarifying context.** When a client's materials contradict each other (brand doc vs. live product), the discipline is to surface every contradiction as a direct question before writing a single strategy line.
- Voice fusion for personal/creative writing: Gary Halbert (direct, punchy, conversational), Mahmoud Darwish (concrete imagery), Sylvia Plath (raw emotional precision), Edgar Allan Poe (rhythm/repetition). No em dashes, no "the kind that," no filler transitions, no AI-sounding language.
- Content voice for brand/thought-leadership work (Reply.cash / DeFi Foundry house style, reused across engagements):
  - Direct — get to the point immediately.
  - Insight-first — lead with the interesting thought, not setup.
  - Calm authority — confident without hype.
  - No calls to action, no emojis, no em dashes, no sales language.
  - 1–3 lines max for short-form; starts from a real infrastructure problem; uses real numbers/data where possible.
  - LinkedIn: 150–300 words, insight-first, no emojis, no CTA.

## Reply.cash — Stablecoin-to-Mobile-Money Infrastructure (6 African markets)

**Approach:** Built a *systematic content production system* — a Notion database schema holding the full permutation space of the product's use cases (Chain × Country × Payment Rail × Format) — then generated content methodically against that matrix.

**Database structure:**
- Fields: Title, Chain (Ethereum, Polygon, Base, Solana, Tron, Arbitrum, Cardano, Stellar, NEAR, Bitcoin…), Country (Kenya, Nigeria, Ghana, Uganda, Malawi, DR Congo), Rail (M-Pesa, Paybill, Send Money, Till Number, Pochi, Banks, OPay, PalmPay, MTN, Airtel, AirtelTigo, Telecel, TNM Mpamba, Orange Money), Format (X Post, LinkedIn, Medium, Blog), Status (Draft/Ready/Published), Wallet notes.
- Priority sequencing: started with the highest-traffic combination (top 5 chains × Kenya M-Pesa = 20 pieces across 4 formats each), then expanded outward.
- Guide format per topic: X/Twitter post (short), LinkedIn article (professional/deeper), Medium article (technical deep-dive with security considerations), Blog post (step-by-step with prerequisites, troubleshooting, FAQ).
- Iterated to 36+ guides live in Notion, tagged and filterable by all four dimensions.

**Course-correction moment:** When platform-format guides came back wrong (X posts too short, LinkedIn posts too long), the fix was asking for a reference example of the "correct" format before mass-correcting; once no example was available, applied stated platform best practices (X ~250–280 chars, LinkedIn ~1,500–2,000 chars) uniformly across the whole backlog.

**Editorial restructuring:** Guides were restructured company-wide to a template-first, action-oriented format: condensed intros, numbered steps prominent, cost tables added — applied across the whole database rather than per-article.

**Coordination:** ClickUp workspace (90121519437) with colleagues Able Cookey and Mark Ajekevwoda.

**Thought leadership:** LinkedIn content deliberately frames stablecoins as **settlement infrastructure, not speculation** — a positioning choice reused later for DeFi Foundry and Architecture of Leverage content.

## DeFi Foundry — Web3 Venture Studio

**Approach:** Built a structured content *menu* first, then let the client select from it.

1. **Long-form (8 topics)** — mega-threads/LinkedIn articles (within 3,000-char limit): Operational Due Diligence, Tokenomics vs. Economics, The Venture Studio Advantage, Zero-to-One in Web3, Market-Making for Founders, The Institutional Bridge, Community Architecture, The GTM of 2026 ("Growth Networks" over traditional marketing).
2. **Short-form (7 topics)** — viral/hot-take tweets: "The Retention Truth," "The Silent Builder," "The Liquidity Lie."
3. **Meme concepts (paired opposites)** — e.g. "Hype Train" vs. "The Solid Foundation," "Founder burnout from hiring" vs. "Studio-accelerated growth."

**Sequencing logic:**
- Week 1 – Foundation & Philosophy: "Silent Builder" → Zero-to-One → Hype vs. Foundation meme.
- Week 2 – Economics & Sustainability: "Simplicity is Power" → "Retention Truth" → Community Architecture.
- Week 3 – Liquidity & Markets: "The Liquidity Lie" → Market-Making for Founders → Institutional Bridge.
- Week 4 – Growth & Execution: GTM-of-2026 → studio-positioning meme.
- Rationale: open with philosophy to earn trust, move to economics to prove rigor, use liquidity content to demonstrate expertise, only then pitch growth/GTM.

**Reuse discipline:** Searched back through prior Reply.cash conversations to re-derive the "Tweet Writing Style" principles rather than re-inventing tone, then confirmed sequencing/tone preference with the client before drafting.

**Founder content:** Award-announcement threads for Machala (founder) went through multiple rejected drafts — structured, "strategic-note"-heavy drafts read as corporate. Fix: re-apply the Halbert/Darwish/Plath/Poe voice framework combined with the Twitter-discipline rules.

## 4Herfrika — Women's Development Organization

## EportalNet / Hunnovate — School Management SaaS (Ondo State)

**Approach:** Built a full 10-section GTM strategy document before any field activity, then executed field canvassing against it and fed findings back into the strategy.

**Strategy document contents:**
- **Market sizing:** TAM/SAM/SOM; Nigerian school-software pricing benchmarks (₦30k–₦800k/yr).
- **ICP:** primary = independent private school proprietor in Southwest Nigeria; secondary = group-of-schools operators. Buying committee: proprietor, head teacher, bursar, IT teacher.
- **Competitive landscape:** SchoolsFocus, eSchool.ng, SchoolRex, BlueBic, Nersapp — each mapped head-to-head with EportalNet's specific edge against it.
- **Core strategic argument:** real defensible edge is *local proximity* (Ondo HQ) + results-first UX mapped to the highest-pain task (result processing).
- **Positioning statement:** "For Nigerian private school proprietors and administrators who are losing hours every term to manual result processing and scattered records, EportalNet is the school management platform that digitises your entire school operation... so you run a tighter, faster, and more professional school without needing an IT department."
- **Four messaging pillars:** Speed ("Process your results in minutes, not days"), Safety (records/backup), Professionalism (school image), Simplicity.
- **Pricing:** 4-tier (Free → ₦48k → ₦96k → ₦180k/yr), aligned to the Nigerian school term cycle.
- **5 GTM channels:** field sales, WhatsApp referrals, education-stakeholder partnerships (NAPPS, church school networks), social media, SEO.
- **90-day plan:**
  - *Month 1:* land-and-expand in Akure, 3–5 paid conversions, initiate NAPPS conversation.
  - *Month 2:* expand to Osun/Oyo; formalize partnership; Facebook ads timed to Sept/Oct result season; 3–5 testimonials; named case study; WhatsApp broadcasts. Goal: 50+ schools, first partnership, 2 testimonials.
  - *Month 3:* double down on best-converting channel; Lagos outreach begins; "Group of Schools" package; directory/G2 submissions; Southwest education expo; full performance review. Goal: 150+ schools, ₦1.2M+ MRR.
- **12-month KPI arc:** Month 1 → 20 schools; Month 12 → 500+ schools, ₦6M+ MRR.

**Field execution:** canvassed 7 schools in Akure, documented in a formatted field report. Key insight: a visit to a *public* school (Oyemekun Grammar) with a strong alumni network/PTA revealed a second entry route — alumni associations/PTAs influence infrastructure decisions, so public-school pitches were reframed as an *alumni-driven school development initiative*, not a vendor pitch.

**Creative tactics (30-item list, standouts):**
- "Results Day" emotional content timed to Sept/Jan result seasons.
- "Before & After" WhatsApp challenge — old paper registers next to the EportalNet dashboard.
- "EportalNet Certified School" badge after one full academic session, displayed on gate banners/prospectuses.
- Team's own top-priority picks: flash onboarding at term-end + LGA proprietors' meetings + a between-terms community group, chosen because they compound.

**Supporting infrastructure:** school directory expanded to ~990 schools across 19 LGAs to feed field-sales/outreach directly.

## BCP Origins — Annual Conference (bcporigins.com)

**Approach:** Template methodology later re-applied to ZendPay — "systematic, not impulsive, ask questions before building." Full historical archive ingested before planning (origin story, 2022–2025 editions, operational structure, partnerships, program flows) — explicitly waited for all files before processing.

**Historical pattern:** 2022 (~150 attendees, Akure) → 2023 (target 3,000, "Building global brands locally") → 2024 ("Accessing Global Opportunities") → 2025 ("From Zero to Global Talent") shifted to an independently-hosted regional model (Birmingham UK, Akure, Kaduna, Ife, Calabar ran; several, including Lagos, cancelled).

**2026 strategic bet:** go small and exclusive — BCP Lagos as a "town hall" / dinner format for key Nigerian stakeholders, themed **TBoT ("The Beginning of Tomorrow")**. Purpose is **market access, not reach** — a buffer event to earn individual relationships that compound into collective attention later.

**Core tension engineered on purpose:** generate public buzz/conversation volume while keeping the actual invitation list exclusive — visibility without access, used to build desirability.

**Narrative discipline:** one single story told consistently across every format (written, audio, images, audiovisual), with a defined individual role per team member for carrying it.

**Voice requirement:** mirror BCP's own written voice/language/grammar from source docs, rather than imposing an external house style.

**Team structure:** Central Leadership Team + Regional Host Teams; Stephen holds Brand & Communications Lead on the CLT (alongside Success Moranti). Historical sponsorship tiers: ₦30k–₦1.5M; past raise target ~$7,000 (₦5.25M).

### TBoT 2026 Marketing Plan — live tactical detail
- **Core line:** *"Somewhere in Lagos, a small room is deciding what the next ten years of African talent looks like. You will hear about it. You may not be in it."* The conversation is public. The table is not.
- **Concept — "Town Criers":** many voices, many places, one question, unconnected until it connects. Rings: Us first (CLT) → The family (past speakers/hosts, suggestion not dependency) → Borrowed mouths (podcasters/press, pitch the conversation not the event) → Paid (small money, only behind content already moving, never first).
- **The one question:** "What is the beginning of tomorrow?" Every piece of content must tie back to it or it doesn't ship.
- **Channels:** Instagram + X primary; Medium/Substack for full-length tellings; WhatsApp voice notes/clips for organic forwarding.
- **Uche:** Uche (deceased) told Toluwase to make the virtual thing physical — the origin of the whole event. Told once, truthfully, not turned into recurring content; nothing ships on this without Toluwase's sign-off.
- **Phasing:** Foundations (now, quiet — lock question/visual identity, gather archive footage) → August ("the question in the air," no logo/event name, weekly voice notes + team posts + archive films) → Early September ("the connection" — reveal wave: essay, origin mini-doc, first criteria card, no registration link, one contact line) → Mid–late September ("the faces" — confirmed guest profiles, team essays, prep clip) → Oct 1–10 (one empty-table post, then event-day fragments, guests carry the rest).
- **Governing rule (from the 2-month Comms & Marketing Strategy doc):** no guest/speaker identity goes out before the event — everything pre-event is retrospective or concept-forward, never person-tied. Full reveal reserved for the post-event campaign.
- **Two campaigns:** Campaign 1 "The Crying" (Aug 2–Oct 10, retrospective/vision only) and Campaign 2 "The Return" (Oct 11 onward, the post-event payoff — carries equal creative weight to the 8 weeks before it).
- **Content engines:** Call for Stories (sourced from core team, past speakers, attendees/regional hosts, people impacted); The Documentary (Week 0 through one week post-event).
- **Content arcs running in parallel:** Toluwase's arc (spine, full campaign), Call for Stories (weeks 0–5), Team angle (weeks 1–7), Archive videos (weeks 1–4), The Room (weeks 5–8), The Reveal (post-event only), Documentary (Week 0 → after).

## ZendPay — Telegram Money Assistant (zendpay.xyz)

**Approach:** Re-ran the BCP methodology — audit everything first, surface every contradiction, ask before building.

**Materials audited:** live site, ZEND Brand Strategy & Guidelines v1.0 (AI-generated via Kimi.ai), color reference guide, Content Plan v2.0 (84 posts/month at 3/day), Content Production Brief. Two uploaded content-plan files were diffed to confirm they were identical besides formatting before treating either as authoritative.

**Contradictions surfaced as clarifying questions:**
1. **Naming:** brand doc says "Zend / zend.money"; live product is "ZendPay / zendpay.xyz / @gozendpay." Which is canonical?
2. **Photography:** brand doc bans faces/stock photography/"smiling Africans holding phones," but the live site runs entirely on lifestyle/testimonial photography with faces. Deliberate override, or guideline never adopted?
3. **Claims and proof:** content plan has specific checkable numbers ("₦50M+ processed," "98.7% voice accuracy") and named testimonials, while the site itself says "open beta" — flagged as a credibility risk for a money product; asked whether the figures are real or placeholders.
4. **Crypto visibility:** brand doc bans "wallet"/"blockchain"/Solana-language, but the live receive-flow shows a visible Solana address and named on-chain partners — asked whether crypto-native users are actually a target audience.

**Clarifying questions asked before proceeding:** actual mandate (full GTM vs. fixing the existing content system vs. both); truthful current product state (real user/transaction numbers, live vs. roadmap); whether the Kimi-generated brand doc is adopted law or a draft; the single primary 90-day success metric; who actually executes (designer availability, account ownership, whether 3 posts/day is realistic).

Strategy work intentionally paused pending answers — never draft content or calendars on top of an unresolved contradiction.

## Architecture of Leverage — Personal Brand (Stephen's Own)

**Framework (as briefed, verbatim structure):**
- **Voice:** calm, precise, contrarian when warranted; authority through insight, not volume.
- **Core belief system:** wealth through positioning and systems, not luck; attention precedes capital; personal brand as a leverage engine.
- **Content must:** teach a non-obvious principle, reframe a misunderstood concept, or reveal how "smart money" actually thinks — without tutorial language or hype.
- **Four content angles:** Actionable (leverage points), Analytical (pattern recognition), Aspirational (what's possible), Anthropological (incentives and psychology).
- **Structure discipline:** hook challenges an assumption → body builds momentum through logic → close reframes thinking.

**Weekly planning approach:** clarifying questions before building a plan — full 7-day calendar vs. 3–5 hero pieces vs. one theme with multiple angles; pure brand-building vs. tied to a launch; format mix; audience temperature. Resulting theme: **"The Architecture of Leverage"** — personal brand and capital allocation framed as parallel, compounding systems.

**Award-announcement case study:** an initial draft sequencing 2024 awards was rejected as not matching his voice — "too structured/corporate, over-explained, lost the conversational punch, felt like marketing not honest writing." Rebuilt by fusing the personal writing-voice framework with the Twitter-discipline framework.
- **Deployment options offered:** 5-day rollout (Day 1 DeFi Foundry credibility → Day 2 Web3/blockchain domain authority → Day 3 entrepreneurship/capital range → Day 4 "systematic builder, not opportunistic winner" synthesis → Day 5 unexpected awards + gratitude close) vs. compressed 3-day intensive (two tweets/day, same order). Governing principle: "You're not announcing 'I won awards' (vanity). You're demonstrating 'the thesis compounds' (authority)."

## Earlier / One-Off Engagements

### Refresh — Career & Wellness Summit (Nov 2025–Mar 2026)
**Strategic frame:** built on a single audience insight — *"I know I'm tired, but I'm scared to slow down — because what if stopping means I lose everything I've worked for?"* Strategic shift: **from** "non-stop grinding is the only way to succeed" **to** "not having a pause means breaking down along the way."
- **Big idea:** "Reset to Fire On!" — holds both rest and reignite.
- **Four pillars:** Pay Raise, Soft Work, Living Fulfilled, Work-Life Balance.
- **Five-phase rollout:** Teaser (X conversation-starter, mystery IG posts) → Official Reveal (Dec 1–20, launch video, press release, landing page) → #SoftWorkChallenge (Jan 5–31, piggybacked on Falz's "Soft Work" track, UGC challenge, top-10 free tickets) → Early Bird/Registration Drive (Feb 1–27, scarcity, speaker reveals, paid ads/OOH) → Conversion/Paid Push (WhatsApp/email follow-up targeting unpaid registrants, corporate outreach) → Event Day (live content, vox pops, activations) → Post-Event (testimonial series, ongoing "Monthly Refresh" series, persistent community channel).
- **Budget (₦):** influencer/#SoftWorkChallenge 500,000; design/creative (3mo) 450,000; paid ads (2mo) 500,000; email/CRM 100,000; PR 350,000; OOH ~5,600,000; merchandise ~2,850,000 + VAT ~3,200,000.
- Deliverable: full multi-phase caption guide with platform variations + engagement-response scripts, built as a living document.
