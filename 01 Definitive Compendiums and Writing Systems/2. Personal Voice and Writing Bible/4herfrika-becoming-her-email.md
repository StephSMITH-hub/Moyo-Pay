# Becoming Her: Mentorship Launch Email

**Subject:** Now you know who to call.

---

For two years, the same question kept coming back to us. In surveys. After cohorts. In DMs at odd hours of the night.

*What happens after this? Who do I call now?*

And every time, we gave the best answer we had. A resource list. A workshop. A "keep going, you're doing great." All of it true. Still short of what you were actually asking for.

Because that question is really about a person. Someone who has stood exactly where you're standing, made the call you're about to make, got it wrong once, got it right the next time, and is willing to tell you both stories.

So we built that.

It's called **Becoming Her**, and it's live today.

Here's how it works. You tell us where you are right now and where you're trying to get to. Not your whole life plan, just the honest version. Then we match you with one woman who has already walked something close to that road.

One mentor, matched to you. Yours. Someone who stays after the lights go off.

**Some of the women you might meet:**

- **Odunola**, Business Consultant
- **Tomide**, Mobile Software Engineer
- **Adesewa**, Product Designer
- **Hassanat**, Product Manager

Women doing the exact work you're training for, in rooms a lot of us haven't been invited into yet.

Career Corner is still there, still open, still the place to book a single call whenever you need one. Becoming Her is for the other thing: the thing where you need someone who remembers your last conversation, who asks how it went, who's still around when the second question shows up. It always does.

We tested this quietly for two seasons before saying a word about it, because we wanted to be sure it held before you put your weight on it.

It held.

So if you've ever finished a bootcamp, closed your laptop, and thought *okay, now what*, this is your now-what.
