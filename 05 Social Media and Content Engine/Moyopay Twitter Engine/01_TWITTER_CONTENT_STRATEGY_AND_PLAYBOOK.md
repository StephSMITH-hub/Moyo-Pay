## 4. THE STEPHYCOPY BRAND VOICE GOVERNANCE ON X

All copy must pass the strict verbal constitution:

1. **TOTAL BAN ON EM DASHES**:
   - Never use `—`. Replace with a full stop, colon, comma, or clean line break.
2. **ZERO AI-SOUNDING JARGON**:
   - Banned: *delve, navigate, landscape, harness, leverage (as a verb), game-changer, paradigm shift, cutting-edge, tapestry, foster*.
   - Replace with physical, grounded words: *dig, run, clear, deploy, build, prove, settle*.
3. **TERMINOLOGY CONTROL**:
   - Always write: **digital dollar wallet**, NEVER *crypto wallet*.
   - Always write: **stable digital dollars / USDT / USDC**, NEVER *coins / tokens*.
   - Always write: **Pioneers / Earners / Builders**, NEVER *users / customers*.
   - Always write: **financial sovereignty**, NEVER *financial inclusion*.
4. **SINGLE-LINE VERDICTS STAND ALONE**:
   - When an argument hits its peak, drop a single sentence separated by empty whitespace above and below.
5. **RADICAL RESPECT FOR LIVED AFRICAN REALITY**:
   - No pity. No charity framing. Celebrate African competence and dismantle outdated banking tolls.
