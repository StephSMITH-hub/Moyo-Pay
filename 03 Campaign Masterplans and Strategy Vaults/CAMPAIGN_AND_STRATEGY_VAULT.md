## Section 2: Market Sizing & Regional Unit Economics
* **Total Addressable Market (TAM)**: All private primary and secondary educational institutions in Nigeria (~85,000 private schools nationwide).
* **Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM)**: Private secondary and primary schools in Southwest Nigeria (Ondo, Ekiti, Osun, Oyo, Ogun, Lagos) operating with at least 150 enrolled students (~18,500 schools).
* **Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM - 12 Months)**: Private schools within Ondo, Ekiti, and Osun states (~2,200 schools across 38 LGAs).

## The Strategic Bet: Market Access Over Mass Reach
Traditional event marketing attempts to sell as many tickets as possible to the widest audience.
