Website Makeovers

Before & After: Turning Confusing Sites into Sales

See how I take generic, boring websites and rewrite them to clearly explain the value and win more clients.

❌ Before: Confusing & Generic Original Website
The Problem: Generic greeting ("Welcome to our firm") and stock photos of gavels. Zero explanation of why a startup founder should hire them. Visitors leave in seconds.
✅ After: Clear & Persuasive New Copy & Layout
The Fix: Clear, bold promise right at the top: "We protect founders from bad contracts before they sign." Simple intake form and clear services that make it easy to book a call.
❌ Before: Vague Freelancer Platform Original Homepage
The Problem: "Find professionals for your project." Sounds identical to Upwork and gives busy clients zero reason to trust them.
✅ After: Trusted Talent Marketplace New High-Converting Design
The Fix: Rebuilt around speed and trust: "Hire pre-vetted tech talent in 48 hours without payroll headaches." Clear and direct for hiring managers.
❌ Before: Boring Agency Pitch Original Agency Site
The Problem: "We build modern software for ambitious companies." Generic corporate fluff that 10,000 other agencies say.
✅ After: Clear Founder-Focused Message New Headline & Positioning
The Fix: Focused on what founders actually care about: "We build and launch your software in 3 weeks so you can start getting users before your budget runs out."
