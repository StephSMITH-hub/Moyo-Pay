## 4. Stephycopy Writing & Voice Guidelines Summary

1. **No Em Dashes**: Banned completely. Use full stops, commas, colons, or line breaks.
2. **No Negation Constructions**: State what the system IS and what it produces.
3. **No "The Kind That" Constructions**: Cut immediately.
4. **No Fluffy Transitions**: Eliminate phrases like *"It is worth noting that..."*, *"In other words..."*, *"At the end of the day..."*.
5. **No AI-Sounding Filler**: Banned words: *delve, landscape, harness, leverage, multifaceted, paradigm shift, game-changer, zero fluff*.
6. **Sentence Cadence**: Short punchy sentences mixed with rhythmic momentum. Periods act as hammers.
7. **Value First, Always**: Expertise is demonstrated through specificity, numbers, and texture, never through self-praise.
